Ekon Academy

We believe that everyone has the right to upgrade themselves and improve their future.

Ekon Academy, a venture of Ekon Solutions India Pvt Ltd, was established to provide students in tier 2, tier 3 cities and rural areas with the same quality training as their counterparts in metropolitan and Tier 1 cities. We offer a range of popular courses in IT sector, empowering students and professionals to upskill themselves and prepare for tough competition. We offer comprehensive learning options, hands-on projects, and courses with guaranteed placement assistance. We have a team of experienced and highly qualified trainers who are passionate about teaching and providing quality education. Our state-of-the-art facilities and modern infrastructure provide the most conducive environment for learning and growth.

Since our inception, we have placed over 1,000 students in jobs across a variety of industries. Through quality education and training, we strive to be one of the leading institutes in the country.
